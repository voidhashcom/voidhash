---
title: Set your raw Google events forwarding URL
description: Choose the backend endpoint that receives forwarded Google Play notifications.
---

**Raw Google events forwarding URL** is optional. Use it when your backend needs a copy of the
raw Google Play notification payloads received by Voidhash.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Get your backend endpoint

Use the URL your team has set up to receive Google Play events, for example
`https://example.com/google-raw-events`. Your backend team supplies this value; Google Play
Console does not generate it.

</Step>

<Step>
### Enter the forwarding URL

Paste the complete endpoint URL into **Raw Google events forwarding URL** and save the provider.
Leave this field empty if you do not need a separate event copy.

</Step>

</Steps>

Forwarding applies to notifications Voidhash receives. Setting this URL does not configure
Google Play or subscribe your backend to a Pub/Sub topic. See the
[RTDN topic guide](/docs/guides/payment-providers/google-play/real-time-developer-notifications)
for topic setup.
