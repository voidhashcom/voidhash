---
title: Find your Google Play RTDN topic name
description: Create or find the Pub/Sub topic used for real-time developer notifications.
---

The optional **Google Play RTDN topic name** field stores the topic you use when managing
notification delivery outside Voidhash. Entering a topic here does not create a Pub/Sub subscription.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Find or create a topic

In [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com), select your project and open
**Pub/Sub → Topics**. Open your RTDN topic or create one.
Copy its full name, such as `projects/example-project/topics/google-play-rtdn`.

</Step>

<Step>
### Allow Google Play to publish

In the topic's permissions, grant `google-play-developer-notifications@system.gserviceaccount.com`
the **Pub/Sub Publisher** role.

</Step>

<Step>
### Configure your app in Play Console

Open your app in [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console), then go to
**Monetize → Monetization setup → Real-time developer notifications**. Enable notifications,
enter the full topic name, choose the notification types, send a test message, and save.

</Step>

<Step>
### Copy the topic to Voidhash

Paste the same full name into **Google Play RTDN topic name** and save. Configure a Pub/Sub
subscription for your backend and confirm it receives the test message.

</Step>

</Steps>

Follow Google's [RTDN setup guide](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/getting-ready#configure-rtdn)
for subscription delivery options and troubleshooting.
