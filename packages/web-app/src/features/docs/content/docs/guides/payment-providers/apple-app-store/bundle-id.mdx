---
title: Find your App Bundle ID
description: Locate the Bundle ID for your app in App Store Connect or Xcode so it matches your Voidhash configuration.
---

Your **App Bundle ID** is the reverse-domain identifier for your app, for example
`com.yourcompany.yourapp`. It must match the bundle identifier in your Xcode project and the
App ID registered in your Apple Developer account.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open App Store Connect

Go to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com), sign in, open **Apps**, and
select your app.
</Step>

<Step>
### Open App Information

In the sidebar choose **General → App Information**, then locate the **Bundle ID** field. It
shows the reverse-domain value, not the numeric **Apple ID** listed on the same page.
</Step>

<Step>
### Copy the identifier

Copy the value (e.g. `com.yourcompany.yourapp`) and paste it into the **App Bundle ID** field
in Voidhash.

<Callout type="info" title="Prefer Xcode?">
You can also find it in Xcode: select your app under **TARGETS**, then read the **Bundle
Identifier** in the **General** tab under **Identity** (it also appears in **Signing &
Capabilities**). The two must be identical.
</Callout>
</Step>

</Steps>
