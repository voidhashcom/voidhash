---
title: Find your App Apple ID
description: Find the numeric Apple ID that identifies your app in App Store Connect.
---

Your **App Apple ID** is the numeric identifier Apple generates for your app. It is usually around
10 digits, for example `1234567890`. Voidhash uses it to reference your app in Apple's APIs. It is
not the same as your Bundle ID, and it is not your personal Apple Account login.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open App Store Connect

Go to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com), open **Apps**, and select your
app.

</Step>

<Step>
### Open App Information

In the sidebar, choose **General → App Information**. The read-only **Apple ID** is listed among
your app's properties, next to your Bundle ID. Apple generates this number automatically and you
cannot edit it.

</Step>

<Step>
### Copy the number

Copy the digits exactly, with no spaces, and paste them into the **App Apple ID** field in
Voidhash.

If your app is already live, there is a shortcut. The App Apple ID also appears in the app's App
Store URL, for example `https://apps.apple.com/app/id1234567890`. The number after `id` is your
App Apple ID.

</Step>

</Steps>
