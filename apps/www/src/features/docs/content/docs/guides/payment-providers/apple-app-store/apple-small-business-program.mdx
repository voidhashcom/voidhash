---
title: Find your Small Business Program dates
description: Determine the Start date and optional End date for your Apple App Store Small Business Program enrollment so Voidhash's commission math matches Apple's reduced rate.
---

The **Start date** is when your reduced ~15% App Store commission under the Apple Small Business
Program became effective, and the **End date** is when it ended if you left or no longer qualify.
Apple does not show these as fields in App Store Connect, so you derive the Start date from your
enrollment approval, and leave the End date blank unless the standard rate has resumed.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Confirm eligibility and enroll

Sign in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com) as the **Account Holder**. You
qualify if you (and any associated accounts) made up to 1,000,000 USD in proceeds in the prior
calendar year, or you are new to the App Store. Review the
[Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/) page and
submit the enrollment form via the **Learn More** block on the App Store Connect home page.
</Step>

<Step>
### Accept the Paid Apps agreement

The program requires the current Paid Apps agreement to be active. In App Store Connect choose
**Business** at the top of the page, open the **Agreements** tab, find the **Paid Apps** row, and
click **View and Agree to Terms**. Its status must show **Active**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Capture the approval date from Apple's email

After you submit, Apple emails a confirmation and later an approval email when you are accepted.
Record the date in that approval email — Apple does not expose an enrollment date or effective-start
field anywhere in the App Store Connect UI.

<Callout type="warn" title="The approval date is not the start date">
The reduced rate is not effective on the approval date. Apple adjusts your proceeds fifteen days
after the end of the fiscal calendar month in which your enrollment is approved — for example,
approved February 10, 2022 means proceeds adjust starting March 14, 2022.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Verify the rate and enter the Start date

Confirm the 15% rate is live before trusting the computed date: download your app's pricing/proceeds
report and check that Year 1 and Year 2 proceeds are equal and roughly 15% below the list price. Use
the date the reduced rate first appears as the authoritative value, then paste it (a plain
month/day/year) into the **Start date** field in Voidhash.
</Step>

<Step>
### Leave the End date blank unless you left the program

There is no fixed end date and no annual re-confirmation — membership carries over each calendar year
while you stay eligible and keep the Paid Apps agreement active. Set the **End date** in Voidhash only
if you exceeded 1,000,000 USD in proceeds this calendar year (the standard rate then applies to future
sales) or you formally left, using the date the standard rate resumed.
</Step>

</Steps>
