---
title: Find your App Apple ID
description: Locate the numeric Apple ID that identifies your app in App Store Connect.
---

Your **App Apple ID** is the numeric identifier Apple automatically generates for your app
(commonly around 10 digits, for example `1234567890`). It is different from your Bundle ID and
from your personal Apple Account login, and is used by Voidhash to reference your app in
Apple's APIs.

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open App Store Connect

Go to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com), open **Apps**, and select your
app.
</Step>

<Step>
### Open App Information

In the sidebar choose **General → App Information**. On this page the read-only **Apple ID** is
listed among your app's properties, alongside your Bundle ID.

<Callout type="info" title="Which Apple ID?">
Here **Apple ID** means your app's numeric identifier — not your personal Apple Account login.
Apple auto-generates it, and you can't edit it.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Copy the number

Copy the digits exactly (no spaces) and paste them into the **App Apple ID** field in Voidhash.

<Callout type="info" title="Shortcut">
If your app is already live, the App Apple ID also appears in its App Store URL:
`https://apps.apple.com/app/id1234567890` — the number after `id` is your App Apple ID.
</Callout>
</Step>

</Steps>
